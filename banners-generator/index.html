Why add a banner to your email signature?

Every email your team sends is free media space. A signature banner turns that space into a channel: promote a campaign, an event, a report or open roles to every person you already write to.

Unlike ads, a signature banner reaches people mid-conversation, with your brand right next to it. It is one of the cheapest marketing surfaces a company has - most teams simply never use it.

The right banner size for Gmail signatures

A good email signature banner is wide and short: 450 x 100 px is the sweet spot recommended by this generator. Stay under 600 px wide - that is the safe content width in most mail clients, including Gmail and Outlook.

Export as PNG for pixel-perfect rendering everywhere, or use the HTML variant for color and gradient banners - it stays sharp on every screen and you can edit the text later without redesigning the image.

Banner generator FAQ

What size should a banner in a Gmail signature be?

450 x 100 px is a safe, good-looking default and what this generator recommends. Keep the width under 600 px so the banner never overflows the message on desktop or mobile, and keep the file small - PNG banners of this size are typically well under 100 KB.

Is this email signature banner generator free?

Yes. The generator is free and runs entirely in your browser - your photos are never uploaded to a server. You do not need a SignatureCat account to design and export banners.

Should I use the PNG or the HTML version of my banner?

PNG works in every email client and is the safest choice, especially for photo banners. The HTML version (available for color and gradient backgrounds) stays razor sharp on every screen and its text remains editable, but gradients can fall back to a solid color in some clients, such as Outlook.

How do I add the banner to my Gmail signature?

In Gmail open Settings, then See all settings, and scroll to Signature. Insert the downloaded PNG with the image button, or paste the copied HTML banner directly into the signature box. To roll banners out to a whole company at once, use a signature manager like SignatureCat.
